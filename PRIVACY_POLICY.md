# Privacy Policy for HabitFlow

**Effective Date:** 2026-01-11

## 1. Introduction
HabitFlow ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the HabitFlow mobile application (the "App").

**HabitFlow is a local-first application.** We do not collect, store, or share your personal data on our servers. All your data remains on your device.

## 2. Information We Collect
We do not collect any personal information, usage data, or analytics from you.
- **Personal Data:** We do not ask for or store your name, email, or phone number.
- **Habit Data:** Your habits, completion history, and goals are stored locally on your device using your phone's internal storage.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
- **Local Storage:** All app data is stored on your device via `AsyncStorage` and the local file system.
- **No Cloud Sync:** Currently, HabitFlow does not sync your data to any cloud server.
- **Backups:** You can manually export your data to a JSON file for backup purposes. This file is stored where you choose (e.g., your device downloads or a cloud drive you manually select). You are responsible for the security of these backup files.

## 4. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with third-party analytics or advertising services that track you.
- **Permissions:** The app may request permissions (e.g., Notifications) solely to provide core functionality (reminders). These are not used for tracking.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Since we do not collect any personal data, our App is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

## 6. Meaning of "Delete Data"
Functionality within the app to "Reset Data" or "Delete Habit" permanently removes that data from your local device storage. Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered unless you have a manual backup.

## 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
